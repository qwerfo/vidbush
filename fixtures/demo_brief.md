A four minute documentary on how the Panama Canal was built. Open on the
voyage it shortened, follow the failed French attempt and the disease that
killed thousands, then the American effort under Gorgas, Stevens and
Goethals: mosquito control, the Culebra Cut, Gatun Dam and the great locks.
End on the 1914 opening, the price paid in money and lives, and the handover
to Panama in 1999. Calm, clear narration with maps, numbers and archival
photographs.
